15-Minute Garlic Noodles ($1.40 a Serving)

⏱ 15 min 🍽 2 servings 💰 ~$2.80 total

No fancy sauces, no long ingredient list — just noodles, garlic, and a few pantry staples you probably already have. This is the recipe to make when it's 9pm, you're broke, and you still want something that actually tastes good.

15 min
Total time
$1.40
Per serving
6
Ingredients

What you'll need

  • Spaghetti or any long noodle200g
  • Garlic cloves, minced5
  • Butter or neutral oil3 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Black pepper, crackedto taste

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles

    Cook your noodles in salted water until just short of al dente — they'll finish cooking in the sauce. Save half a cup of the starchy pasta water before draining.

  2. Toast the garlic

    While the noodles cook, melt the butter in a pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook slowly, stirring often, until golden and fragrant — about 3 minutes. Don't rush this, burnt garlic turns bitter.

  3. Build the sauce

    Stir in the soy sauce and sugar. Let it bubble for 30 seconds so the sugar dissolves into the garlic butter.

  4. Toss it together

    Add the drained noodles straight into the pan. Toss to coat, adding a splash of the reserved pasta water if it looks dry. Finish with cracked black pepper.

Budget tip

Buy garlic and soy sauce in bulk once — both last for months and get reused across dozens of meals, which is what actually brings the per-serving cost down over time.

Make it your own

Cracked an egg into the pan at the end for extra protein, or added a handful of frozen vegetables while the garlic toasts. A fried egg on top turns this into a full dinner for almost no extra cost.
